**[Fedir Tkach](/en/residents/adults/fedir-tkach)** is a director, screenwriter, theatre pedagogue, and member of the National Union of Theatre Artists of Ukraine. Born in Odesa into a family of philologists, he received theatre directing education in both Odesa and Kyiv. In the late 1990s and early 2000s, he was a well-known showman, KVN performer, and director of Odesa Humoryna festivals, children's festivals, and television programs.

He has dedicated his life to pedagogical work and holds the title "Excellence in Education of Ukraine." For over 20 years, he has been teaching acting at the Odesa Theatre School, nurturing talented masters across various media professions and promoting patriotic initiatives with children and youth. Invariably with Ukraine in his heart! He was the host of the Odesa Orange Maidan, co-author of the lyrics and director of the "Odesa Euromaidan Anthem", and actively volunteers in support of the Armed Forces of Ukraine.

In 2025, he launched the new project **«Tkach-Perekladach» (Tkach-Translator)** dedicated to the adaptation, stage dramatization, and Ukrainian translation of literary masterpieces, as well as the popularization of the Ukrainian Word.

For children's theatre troupes, drama clubs, and studios, the project offers ready-made, adapted stage adaptations of famous fairy tales in Ukrainian:

### Nils's Metamorphosis

**Sparrow:** Look at Nils! Look at Nils!\
**Hen 1:** Cluck-cluck… Throw him in the river!\
**Hen 2:** Serves him right! Serves him right!\
**Goose 1:** Hand-some! Oh, so hand-some!\
**Goose 2:** What, scared now? Scared?

*(They pecked, pinched, beat him with their beaks, pulled his arms and legs. They scatter when the cat appears)*

**Nils:** Dear kitty, you know every corner, every hole, every burrow in our yard. Please tell me where I can find the gnome? He couldn't have gone far.\
**Cat:** *(after a cat pause)* Purr, purr! Of course I know where the gnome is… But whether I will tell you or not remains to be seen…\
**Nils:** Little kitty, purr-baby, you must help me! Don't you see that the gnome bewitched me?\
**Cat:** *(eyes sparkling)* And why on earth should I help you? Perhaps for putting a wasp in my ear? Or for setting my fur on fire? Or for pulling my tail every single day? Huh?\
**Nils:** *(indignantly)* I could pull your tail right now!

*(The cat tackles Nils)*

**Nils:** Help, help!\
**Cat:** *(releases him)* Alright, that's enough for the first time. If your mother weren't such a kind mistress who fed me milk morning and evening, you'd be in serious trouble. For her sake, I'll spare your life.

*(The Infanta blushes)*\
**Duchess:** What beauty!!!\
**Harlequin:** Until now you were like a white rose, and now you are like a red rose.\
**King:** Double the salary!\
**Duchess:** What an honour!!!\
**Harlequin:** I entertain everyone not for money, but from the bottom of my heart!\
**Duchess:** What a disgrace!!!\
**Count:** Your Highness! Allow me to present you with a golden rose.

*(a rose in a vase)*

**Harlequin:** It is clearer than clear that they love each other… Clear as the crystal of this vase.\
**King:** Double the doubled salary!\
**Duchess:** What an honour!!!\

## Repertoire List of Adaptations for Children's Theatre

5. **«The Nightingale»** based on Hans Christian Andersen
6. **«Alice in Wonderland»** based on Lewis Carroll
7. **«Going to the Stars»** based on Gianni Rodari
8. **«Cipollino»** based on Gianni Rodari
9. **«Tales of Mother Meadows»** based on Joel Chandler Harris
10. **«Castle Eulenstein»** based on Otfried Preussler

Furthermore, the project includes numerous adapted texts for poetic drama, adolescent theatre, and classical playwriting.

Exclusive materials also include Ukrainian translations of Hanna Yablonska's play "The Concierge" and Boris Barsky's comedy "Mozart and Salieri".
