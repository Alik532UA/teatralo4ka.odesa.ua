Lately we have had wonderful news from our alumni! 🎬

- The Ukrainian short film “Easter Day” (directed by Mykola Zaseev) won the Golden Dziga film award. Congratulations to our [Hryhorii Naumov](/en/projects/galaxy-graduates/hryhorii-naumov), who played the leading role, and to [Illia Volynets-Trifonov](/en/projects/galaxy-graduates/illia-tryfonov), a co-author of the screenplay!
- Our [Mykhailo Haniev](/en/projects/galaxy-graduates/mykhailo-haniev) made his debut as an assistant director on the theatre project “ΚΑΣΣ_ΑΝΔΡΑ” by the renowned Polish director Grzegorz Jarzyna.
- The Ukrainian film “Silence” (directed by Pavlo Shpehun) became the first ever to receive the new Airone della Laguna award at the Venice Film Festival. The leading role is played by [Hryhorii Naumov](/en/projects/galaxy-graduates/hryhorii-naumov), and one of the producers of the short film is our [Inna Lastochkina](/en/projects/galaxy-graduates/inna-shevchenko).
- Also at the Venice Film Festival, our [Daria Zhuravel](/en/projects/galaxy-graduates/daryna-zhuravel) presented the project of her film “Youth”.

Congratulations! We love you! We are proud of you! 💙💛
