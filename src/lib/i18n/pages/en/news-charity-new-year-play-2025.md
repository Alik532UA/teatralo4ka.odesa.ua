The **Odesa Theatrical School**, together with the NGO "Center for the Development of Modern Culture" and the NGO "PRO.Theater", invited the families of Ukrainian Armed Forces veterans "Veterans HUB ODESA" to the charity performance "New Year's Horseshoe for Luck."

Both children and adults thoroughly enjoyed the New Year's fairy tale and received gifts from Santa Claus 🎅🎉🎄.
