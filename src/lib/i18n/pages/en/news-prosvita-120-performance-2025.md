We were honored to join the landmark event "Prosvita 120"! Together with our partners, the NGO "PRO.Theater" (@go_pro.teatr), we shared this special day and presented our collaborative art.

Our young talents demonstrated incredible sincerity and total dedication on stage. For us, the most important thing is to give children a space for creativity, help them find their voice on stage, and raise a generation that deeply feels culture and is not afraid to create.

Thank you to the organizers for the invitation, the warm words about our performance, and your true friendship! We deeply value our partnership and the shared mission of supporting and developing modern culture in Odesa.

We are so proud of our kids and keep moving forward!
