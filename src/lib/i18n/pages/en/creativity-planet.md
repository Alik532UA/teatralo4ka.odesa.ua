# Planet of Creativity

These are the people studying at the school right now. Every pupil has their own
page: plays and roles, department, art college, and their own story.

Studying at the school? Fill in the form — and you appear on the Planet of
Creativity.

After graduation the page stays: the person moves to the Galaxy of Graduates
together with everything they have told about themselves.
