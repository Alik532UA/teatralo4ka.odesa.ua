The **Odesa Theatrical School** took part in a solemn concert dedicated to the 155th anniversary of the prominent Ukrainian poet Lesya Ukrainka. The event was organized by the Department of Culture of the Odesa City Council.

Lesya's brilliant poems sounded harmoniously and inspiringly within the walls of the Odesa Literary Museum, performed by the talented students of the school's theatrical and musical departments.
