The Odesa Theater School has joined a good cause together with the NGO "Life Will Win". Students of the art and theater departments hand-painted packages that were later used to pack tourniquets for our defenders.

We were extremely pleased to see the report on the delivered bandages — and we are sincerely glad that we could become a part of this important assistance.

We are proud of our children and teachers, and grateful to everyone who joined our common cause 💙💛

Glory to Ukraine! Glory to the Armed Forces of Ukraine!
