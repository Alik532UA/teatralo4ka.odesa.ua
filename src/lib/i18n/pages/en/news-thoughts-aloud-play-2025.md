The poetic play "Thoughts Aloud," based on the poems of Pavlo Vyshebaba, continues to win the hearts of the audience! This creative project was produced by the Odesa Theatrical School with the partnership support of our own project, the NGO "PRO.Theater" (@go_pro.teatr).

We are absolutely thrilled with the play's success and have great news to share: we have been invited to perform at the "Dotyk" creative studio (@dotuk_studio\_).

Thank you all for your support, and we look forward to seeing you at our upcoming shows!
